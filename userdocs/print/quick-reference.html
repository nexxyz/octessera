quick reference

Controls, bindings, behaviors, and Play pages across two pages.

Regular controls

Turn MainNavigate/edit. Numeric: plain coarse; Fn fine; Shift and Shift+Fn stay coarse. Enum, boolean, and text turns stay ordinary.
Click MainMenu selection / select, edit, confirm
BackExit location / exit edit, overlay, or menu level
PlayTransport / play or pause
Shift + PlayStop / external sync arms a one-shot resync
Fn + PlayReset stop / stop, reset position, silence owned notes
Fn + turn Main rightSingle step / advance one tick while paused/stopped
Shift + BackClear layer / re-initialize active layer
Shift + Fn + MainContext help / hold modifiers, click menu item

Navigation + bindings

Fn + left columnNavigate to Layer 1-8 / bottom row = layer 1
Hold FnPreview / layers and Play pages
Fn + right columnTechnical Play pages / Mix, Pan, Route, Tempo, LFO, Faders
Shift + Fn + right columnMusical Play pages / FX, XY, Transpose, Rate, Trigger Gate
Shift + Fn + left columnGate a layer / its triggers off, press again to restore
Fn + Aux clickBind focused value/action / value to turn, action to click
Fn + click MainOpen Aux mapping / 12-slot scrollable modal

Aux map: stays open after you release Fn; turn Main to scroll, click Main or press Back to close.

Auto-map fills unbound Aux turns and clicks for this menu area; manual wins unless Aux Prio. is Auto.

Aux markers: row caps show the Aux (1-3 pips, bar: Shift bank); footer: turn, click with !.

Special grid modes

Sample assignShift + tile maps row; Shift + Fn + tile maps column
(MIDI) Drum assignShift+tile: row; Shift+Fn+tile: column; Tile Tune: one tile
Probability mapNever, low, high, always. Shift+tile: row; Shift+Fn+tile: column

Patch orientation

  1. Route sequencer or looper to sampler for a loop; Send MIDI: all inst. doubles it on MIDI.
  2. Add life, raindrops, bounce, or dla to a synth.
  3. Use probability on sequencer tiles so patterns feel alive.
  4. Bind aux encoders to parameters or actions you keep reaching for.

Play pages

MixVolumes; bottom row mutes (never saved). Shift: B1-B4 + master.
PanRows are instruments, left to right; bus-routed ones move their bus pan
RouteRows 1-5: direct or B1-B4. Row 6: FX bypass (again: undo). Top: duck source.
Tempo50-240 BPM ladder, Tap top-left, a -4..+4 nudge row, then Swing A:B (4:4 straight)
LFORow per LFO: on/off, then 1/8 to 8 bars (again: triplet). Shift: depth.
FadersSlider per column, Shift: 8 more. Action Cols: action/step tiles.
FXPress mapped tiles to trigger live effects
XYTwo-axis surface for mapped parameters. Shift + press latches; Glide smooths it.
TransposeSynth, FM, Plucked & enabled MIDI; Sampler/Drum when Follow Transp. is on
RateStep rate per layer. Shift: Scan Unit. Lit tile: resync at the next bar.
Trigger GatePer layer: block, custom probability, or allow

Good to know

Aux turns: numbers coarse, Fn fine; the Shift bank with Shift (Shift+Fn fine). Lists move one option.

Keyboard (by key position): grid rows 1-4 from C F R 4, left Alt 5-8; Aux 123 QWE ASD.

Factory preset hint: life on Layer 1, sequencer rhythm on Layer 2.

Sample browser: .. parent, [folder] folder, (empty) nothing; Play previews. Long names clip or scroll.

Display: System > Display > Home Visual draws the master as scope, spectrum or waterfall on the home page. Signal on an instrument, bus or Global FX fills the screen with that sound; click Main to flip views. Main Font / Small Font: Octessera or Original.

Bench safety: Power through the enclosure USB-C breakout; remove both the board microSD card and OLED microSD card before closing the case.

If the grid behaves unexpectedly, check the OLED. You are probably in an overlay or Play page. Use Back or Fn to leave.
