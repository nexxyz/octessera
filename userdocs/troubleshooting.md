# Troubleshooting

Find the row that matches what you're seeing and start with the first action.
Power down before opening the case, moving wiring or reseating a connector.
And take both microSD cards out before you open or close the case: they catch
on the enclosure and break.

## Sound and playing

| Symptom | First action | More |
|---|---|---|
| No sound from the device | Check that the layer's Link **Inst** points at an instrument that isn't `none`, that it has a level in Play Mix and isn't muted there (red bottom tile), and the cable. The shipped patch (`System > Maintenance > Reset > Factory Patch`) is a known-good starting point. | [Sound and Link](sound-and-link.md), [behaviors](behaviors-and-play.md) |
| A `MIDI Inst.` or `MIDI Drum` instrument stays silent | Switch on its **Enabled** and `System > MIDI > MIDI Active`, and check the channel. A MIDI Drum only sends from tiles you stamped. | [Sound and Link](sound-and-link.md#shape-what-it-sounds-like), [USB roles](hardware/usb-roles.md) |
| Notes hang on external gear | `System > Panic` sends all-notes-off. | |
| A note on the internal synth keeps ringing | **Cause:** the note never got its note-off. Usually the layer's **Off** event isn't set to `note off`, or **Prob Note Off** is on and the dice dropped it.<br>**Check:** `Link > Lx > Events` (Off) and `Trigger Prob. > Prob Note Off`.<br>**Fix:** `System > Panic` silences the internal sounds and sends all-notes-off. Then fix the layer's Off event. | [Sound and Link](sound-and-link.md#events-tiles-changing-state) |
| Crackles or short dropouts | Set `System > Audio > Polyphony` to `hard` first (it keeps the low-latency feel), then thin out dense patches or switch **Perf. Mode** to Capacity. Avoid updates or SSH maintenance while playing. | [Practical performance](performance-and-live.md#practical-performance), [playing in front of people](performance-and-live.md#playing-in-front-of-people) |
| Harsh distortion on loud parts, red `CLIP` in the header | The mix is louder than full scale and the output clamps it. Lower `System > Audio > Master Vol`, or the instruments and buses on the Mix page, until `CLIP` stays away. Stacked drive, resonance and gain add up faster than you'd think. | [Status on the OLED](controls-reference.md#status-on-the-oled), [Sound and Link](sound-and-link.md) |
| Controls or grid respond oddly | Look at the OLED for a Play page or assignment mode, then press **Back** or jump away with **Fn**. | [Controls cheat sheet](controls-cheat-sheet.md) |
| A keyboard does nothing | Set `System > Display > HDMI Video > Mode` to a graphical mode (`Terminal` ignores keyboards). On the Raspberry, a wired keyboard also needs USB Role Host. | [Controls cheat sheet](controls-reference.md#usb-host-keyboard), [Bluetooth](bluetooth.md) |
| A sample slot shows `N/A-<name>` | That WAV isn't on this device, won't decode, or is too long (about two minutes of stereo at most). Copy it into the samples folder, trim it, or pick a replacement. | [Samples](samples.md), [backup and restore](data-backup-restore.md) |
| The SD2 card doesn't show up | It must be FAT32 or exFAT and named exactly `OCTESSERA`. Give it a couple of seconds after inserting. | [Samples](samples.md) |
| Desktop simulator is silent | Check the computer's audio output, then press **Space** with the shipped patch loaded. | [Desktop simulator](desktop-simulator.md#make-a-first-sound) |
| A new build makes no sound | **Cause:** the DAC isn't seated, the cable is in the wrong jack, or the patch is quiet.<br>**Check:** `System > Maintenance > Hardware Test` ends with a test tone. No tone means hardware; a tone means the patch.<br>**Fix:** no tone: power down, reseat the DAC and plug into its audio output. Tone but no music: load `System > Maintenance > Reset > Factory Patch` and press *Play*. | [Assembly manual](hardware/assembly-manual.md#4-open-assembly) |

## Setup and network

| Symptom | First action | More |
|---|---|---|
| Setup hotspot or page doesn't appear | End Live Show Mode if it's on, then open `System > Connections > Wi-Fi > Setup Portal` again. Keep the device powered and don't interrupt an apply in progress. | [Setup portal](hardware/setup-portal.md) |
| Setup finished but the hotspot vanished | That's expected. Reconnect to your normal network; `System > Maintenance > Sys. Info` shows the new address. | [Setup portal](hardware/setup-portal.md) |
| Wi-Fi won't connect | **Cause:** a mistyped password, a network the scan missed, Wi-Fi switched off, or a stuck Wi-Fi chip.<br>**Check:** the OLED message after **Apply setup**, and that `System > Connections > Wi-Fi > Wi-Fi` is On and Live Show Mode isn't running.<br>**Fix:** open the setup portal again and retype the password. Missing network: reopen the portal to rescan; hidden network: **Other network…**. `Wi-Fi chip stopped` needs a restart first. | [Setup portal](hardware/setup-portal.md#what-you-should-see) |
| Forgotten hotspot or device password | **Cause:** the hotspot password is new every time; the device password is the one you set in the portal.<br>**Check:** the hotspot password is on the OLED, on the line below the hotspot name.<br>**Fix:** for the device password, open `System > Connections > Wi-Fi > Setup Portal` on the instrument and set a new one. No old password needed. | [Setup portal](hardware/setup-portal.md) |
| The setup page won't open on a phone | **Cause:** the hotspot has no internet, so the phone may keep using mobile data and never reach the instrument.<br>**Check:** the phone is joined to `Octessera Setup <suffix>`.<br>**Fix:** switch mobile data off for a moment (or tell the phone to stay on the hotspot), then browse to `http://192.168.42.1`. | [Setup portal](hardware/setup-portal.md#steps) |
| No SSH or updates | Check `System > Connections > Wi-Fi > Wi-Fi` is On and Live Show Mode isn't running. | [Playing in front of people](performance-and-live.md#playing-in-front-of-people) |
| An update didn't start and the board went back to the old version | **Cause:** the new version didn't come up within a minute, so the board switched back by itself. The updater logs `v<new> did not start; went back to v<old>`. If it couldn't save your settings first, it logs `Could not save the settings; staying on v<old>`.<br>**Check:** `System > Maintenance > Updates > Check` shows what's installed. For the log, [SSH in](hardware/setup-portal.md#logging-in-with-ssh) and run `journalctl -u 'octessera-update-switch@*' --no-pager`.<br>**Fix:** nothing is lost; you're on the version you had. Try **Apply** again later. | [Updates](updates.md) |
| The computer doesn't see USB audio or MIDI | **Cause:** the wrong port or cable, the Raspberry in Host mode, or the feature switched off.<br>**Check:** Raspberry: `System > Connections > USB Role` is Gadget and the micro-USB data port goes to a USB-A port on the computer (not USB-C to USB-C). Orange: the two-dot USB-C port. Then `System > Audio > USB Audio` and `System > MIDI > USB Device > USB MIDI` are On. While an SD2 transfer runs, the computer sees the card instead.<br>**Fix:** set what the check found, then unplug and replug the cable. | [USB roles](hardware/usb-roles.md), [safety and power](hardware/safety-and-power.md#usb-data) |

## Hardware

| Symptom | First action | More |
|---|---|---|
| The screen is dark, or looks dimmer than it was | **Cause:** the Dim Timer dimmed it, or OLED Sleep switched it off, so a menu doesn't burn into the panel.<br>**Check:** turn or press any knob. The press that wakes the screen is used up waking it, so press again to act. If something failed, the error screen comes back for 10 seconds, then switches itself off again.<br>**Fix:** lengthen `System > Display > Dim Timer` or `OLED Sleep`; `0` turns that timer off. | |
| The OLED says `HARDWARE FAULT` | **Cause:** the grid, keys, screen or encoders stopped answering. The first line names what: `Grid`, `Keys`, `Grid + Keys`, `Key signal`, `Screen`, `I2C bus`, `Encoders`, or `Several parts`. On a Raspberry Pi it can happen, very rarely, after days or weeks without a break; the Orange Pi doesn't do it.<br>**Check:** the second line. `not found` lists what to check: `check grid cable,` (the grid's five-wire cable at both ends), `reseat the NeoKey,` or `reseat the screen,`. `not responding` has nothing to check.<br>**Fix:** unplug the power, count to five, plug it back in. A reboot isn't enough: the grid and key boards only reset when the power really goes away. If it keeps coming back, do the check it names. | [Assembly manual](hardware/assembly-manual.md) |
| The OLED says `CAN'T START`: my saved settings won't load | **Cause:** a saved file that the instrument can't read.<br>**Check:** the screen picks a choice for you: **Last backup** if a recent backup of your patch still loads, otherwise **Restore factory**. Turn a knob to change it, press to confirm (it may need one press to wake the screen).<br>**Fix:** press to restore. Only the unreadable file is replaced, and the old one stays beside it as `….broken-<date>`. Presets, samples and recordings aren't touched. To look first, turn to **Shut down**, press it, and check the SD card on a computer. If the screen says `Restore failed.`, try the other choice or shut down and check the card. | [Backup and restore](data-backup-restore.md) |
| The OLED says `AUDIO NOT READY` | Give it a minute: Octessera retries on its own about every 30 seconds, and a hiccup at startup usually clears itself. If the screen stays for a few minutes, unplug power, check that the DAC sits firmly in its socket, and plug it back in. If it still comes back, the audio setup on the card is the likely culprit: reflash the image (back up your patches first). | [Flash and first boot](hardware/flash-and-first-boot.md), [backup and restore](data-backup-restore.md) |
| The OLED says `STARTUP DELAYED`, or on the Orange Pi `STARTUP FAILED` or `OLED NOT READY` | The instrument didn't finish starting. `STARTUP DELAYED` often clears by itself, so give it a minute. If it stays, or you see one of the other two, unplug the power and plug it back in. If it keeps coming back, [SSH in](hardware/setup-portal.md#logging-in-with-ssh) and run `journalctl -u octessera.service --no-pager`; the last lines say what went wrong. | [Flash and first boot](hardware/flash-and-first-boot.md) |
| The OLED says `Restore failed` (`Pre-restore kept`) | **Cause:** a [restore from a backup](data-backup-restore.md#restore-onto-a-board) went wrong while it ran. Running out of space is one cause.<br>**Check:** the instrument has already put the old data back; the screen says the pre-restore copy was kept.<br>**Fix:** press *Main* on **Close**, free some space, and try the restore again. Keep the downloaded file until you're happy. | [Backup and restore](data-backup-restore.md) |
| The OLED says `Restore blocked` (`Earlier restore unfinished.`) | **Cause:** an earlier restore was interrupted, for example by a power cut, and its leftovers are still on the card.<br>**Check:** nothing was changed this time; your data is as it was.<br>**Fix:** press *Main* on **Close**, restart Octessera so it can finish tidying up the earlier restore, then try again. | [Backup and restore](data-backup-restore.md) |
| Saves fail (`CAN'T SAVE`: `Your patch was not saved.` or `Settings were not saved.`) or a restore says the board has no room | **Cause:** the SD card may be full. A restore that doesn't fit answers `insufficient_space` (HTTP 507).<br>**Check:** [SSH in](hardware/setup-portal.md#logging-in-with-ssh) and run `df -h /`. Recordings are the usual space hogs; see [where files land](recording.md#where-files-land).<br>**Fix:** delete old recordings and samples you don't need, then save again. The error shows once, then a red cross stays in the header where the save tick goes; Auto Save keeps trying quietly, catches up as soon as there's room, and the cross goes. | [Recording](recording.md) |
| Presets are missing after a power pull, a rollback or a reflash | **Cause:** power pull: edits you hadn't saved are gone. Rollback: presets saved on the newer version stay on the card, but the older version can't open them. Reflash: a fresh image starts with only the factory content.<br>**Check:** which of the three happened.<br>**Fix:** power pull: nothing to recover, so save before you unplug. Rollback: press **Rollback** again to go forward. Reflash: restore your backup file. | [Updates](updates.md), [backup and restore](data-backup-restore.md#restore-onto-a-board) |
| One tile, or a whole 4x4 quarter of the grid, stays dark or ignores presses | **Cause:** a quarter is one NeoTrellis board, so the trouble sits with that board: its address jumpers or the pins joining it to its neighbors. A single tile is usually its silicone key or LED.<br>**Check:** `System > Maintenance > Hardware Test` lights every LED and counts every pad.<br>**Fix:** power down. For a quarter, check its jumpers against the table and reflow the joining pins. For one tile, reseat the silicone keypad flat. | [Assembly manual](hardware/assembly-manual.md#neotrellis-and-neokey) |
| OLED is blank, flickering or unstable | Power down and check the display's cable at both ends. If it's a fresh build, also check that the image matches the board and the card is fully seated. | [Assembly manual](hardware/assembly-manual.md) |
| Brownout, reboot, heat or power instability | Power down and check the regulated supply, input, cables and ventilation. | [Safety and power](hardware/safety-and-power.md) |
| The case doesn't fit or a port is blocked | Don't force it. Remove the microSD cards, then check the fit and cable routing. | [Assembly manual](hardware/assembly-manual.md), [enclosure notes](hardware/enclosure.md) |

`System > Maintenance > Hardware Test` walks you through the grid LEDs, every
pad, the four keys, the encoders and a test tone, then tells you what it saw.
It's handy right after assembly, or when one pad seems to have gone quiet. If nothing here matches, stop before changing wiring and
go back to the [flash and first-boot guide](hardware/flash-and-first-boot.md),
[USB roles](hardware/usb-roles.md) or [safety and power](hardware/safety-and-power.md)
page that best fits the problem.
